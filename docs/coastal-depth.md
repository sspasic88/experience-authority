## Sources, access and photography

- The current June 2026 Arcachon brochure was downloaded and all four pages visually checked, including the separate oyster/fishing columns, ports and reservation conditions. The public oyster outing lasts three to six hours depending on port and tide. Visitors follow the professional's direction, with no promised right to handle equipment or independently enter the beds. Older reservation advice was not used to override the current programme.
- Dune du Pilat manager sources distinguish accessible reception facilities from the sand climb and inaccessible summit. The seasonal stairs do not provide step-free access. Current notices, heat and weather remain a pre-visit check. Banc d'Arguin is a separate reserve, not included access.
