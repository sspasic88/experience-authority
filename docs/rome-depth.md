## Why this city and this sequence

Rome closes a major relatability gap without becoming a generic list of landmarks. Pasta is the familiar entry, but the guide stays with dough, shape, filling and sauce rather than a tasting checklist. Via Appia changes the scale from a plate to the infrastructure that carried Rome beyond its centre. Centrale Montemartini completes the sequence with an unexpected but legible encounter between classical sculpture and the machinery of Rome's first public power station.

The chapter does not force all three into one day. The pasta class is a fixed three-hour booking at one of two locations. The Via Appia ride depends on surface, traffic, heat, weather, equipment and the return journey. Centrale Montemartini is a flexible museum visit on Via Ostiense. The page states that there is no combined ticket, transfer, bicycle support or package.
