# Paris depth

13 September 2026. Follows the historical [Kraków release](krakow-depth.md).

## Current edition

This document records the 69-guide Paris release. The current 75-guide scope is documented in [Rome depth](rome-depth.md). Paris has three deliberately different public routes: a substantial croissant and viennoiserie workshop, a guided visit inside the active Gobelins weaving workshops and a self-guided visit through the Paris Sewer Museum.

The chapter begins with a highly recognisable Paris reference and then opens two less expected forms of attention. It does not present the city as a checklist and it does not force all three stops into one day. Each guide remains a separate booking or admission with its own location, timing, language and access conditions.

## Evidence and access boundaries

- Le Cordon Bleu publishes a 5.5-hour Freshly Baked Pastries workshop for adults, taught in French with consecutive English translation. EA does not call it a quick café activity, a neighbourhood secret or a guarantee of participant results.
- The City of Paris and Paris je t'aime publish the Gobelins behind-the-scenes route through two active workshops. Current information points to French delivery, many stairs, uneven surfaces and no late admission. EA does not promise English access or hands-on participation.
- The Paris Sewer Museum publishes a public route through dry interpretation galleries and a wet part of the active network. Operational and flooding conditions can affect access. EA does not encourage independent tunnel exploration or claim to have audited the environment.
- No provider was contacted. No partnership, availability, booking, commission, attendance or local validation is implied.
