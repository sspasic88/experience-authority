# Content expansion batch 24

14 September 2026

## Published

Three original-register candidates were individually reviewed and connected to public-source guides:

- Candidate 85 becomes a route to one confirmed silletero farm in Santa Elena above Medellín. The official directory currently lists nearly seventeen farms, but each host controls its own access and activity. The guide does not promise a mini-silleta workshop, parade participation or unrestricted family and farm access.
- Candidate 3 becomes an ordinary-admission visit to the House of the Carniolan Bee in Višnja Gora. The public promise is the exhibition and close observation of a real glass hive when operating. It does not promise bee handling, apiary access or a workshop.
- Candidate 24 becomes Chios Mastic Museum as a related alternative. The museum's outdoor tree exhibit and indoor cultivation and processing story make mastiha legible without pretending that public admission includes a working harvest or grove access.

All three guides use real and source-specific photography. Each image is locally served, clickable and carries the standard `Image: creator / source ↗` credit. The Medellín image comes from the city's official visitor bureau. The Višnja Gora image is credited to Andrej Peunik through the House of the Carniolan Bee. The Chios image is by Palden Dorenský and is documented under CC BY-SA 4.0. The media register states exactly what each photograph depicts and what it cannot prove.

## Held after review

- Candidate 36, paper making at Konigil near Samarkand, has a credible current public kernel. The exact official photographs reviewed were documentary rather than premium at EA card and hero scale. It remains off the public catalogue until a strong, responsibly creditable photograph and current direct access route can be verified together.
- Candidate 74, a public Cajun or zydeco Sunday dance in Lafayette, has a credible current weekly programme. The official and licensed photographs reviewed were either too weak, too generic or too dated for the current visual system. The programme evidence remains recorded, but the guide is not published yet.

Holding these candidates is not inactivity. It prevents the original list from turning into a volume target that lowers the experience, evidence or visual threshold.

## Discovery and connected value

- 84 public-source guides now cover 44 countries and territories.
- 45 of the original 200 candidates have a dated mapped relationship to a public guide. A mapping may be a narrowed public scope, a related alternative or a protected public-programme boundary. It is not bulk approval or EA Selected status.
- 39 individually authored daily discoveries now run through 21 October 2026.
- Medellín enters `Cities from the inside`, turning a globally recognisable parade image back toward the highland work behind it.
- Chios enters `What a place tastes like`, but the guide itself remains in Learn because ordinary admission does not promise a tasting or hands-on production.
- Višnja Gora remains outside the hands-on pathways. This preserves the difference between watching a living hive and participating in beekeeping.

Each new guide includes a direct official planning route, a practical-fit summary, saving, comparison, journey planning, sharing, correction and a labelled continuation. Wider suggestions are explicitly editorial and never imply proximity.
