# New York City depth — 13 September 2026

## Public scope

78 public-source guides across 38 countries and territories, 11 active fields, 12 collections, nine Journal articles and 14 local planning chapters. New York City opens with three deliberately different public routes: a hands-on bagel class, a museum and factory tour inside an active Brooklyn seltzer works and an escorted Hard Hat Tour through selected hospital buildings on Ellis Island.

## Editorial logic

The chapter begins with one of the city's most recognisable foods, but replaces a ranking with technique. The seltzer works then turns an everyday drink into a system of glass, pressure, return, repair and delivery. Ellis Island provides the more difficult final chapter, adding care, inspection and exclusion to the familiar arrival story. None of the three is presented as a complete account of New York or of the communities and workers behind it.

These are separate bookings in different parts of the city. The bagel venue must be confirmed from the live booking. The seltzer museum is in Cypress Hills and usually offers public tours on selected Fridays. Ellis Island needs a separate ferry ticket, security time, early arrival, closed-toe shoes and careful review of the age and mobility restrictions. The chapter is not a timed route, package, combined ticket or transport plan.

## Photography and rights

All three guides use real images published by the named organisations. BagelUp shows actual class participants with their hand-shaped dough. Brooklyn Seltzer Museum shows the filling line, siphons and machinery on the production floor. Save Ellis Island publishes Emery Roth II's photograph of JR's Unframed installation inside the hospital complex. The two unnamed photographers remain explicitly unnamed. Reuse rights have not been independently cleared and attribution does not create a licence.
