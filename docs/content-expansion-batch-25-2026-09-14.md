# Content expansion batch 25

14 September 2026

## Published

Two original-register candidates were individually reviewed and connected to public-source guides:

- Candidate 28 becomes an ordinary-admission route through Appenzeller Schaukäserei in Stein. Visitors can watch daily production from the gallery, follow the exhibition toward the cellar and use the included tasting after the process is legible. This is a related alternative because the original pasture-to-cellar premise is not fulfilled. Ordinary admission does not include a farm visit or the separately arranged group cheese-making offer.
- Candidate 126 becomes careful public observation of Ajman's Dhow Yard from a clearly public or visitor-permitted edge. Ajman Tourism explicitly says visitors can watch craftsmen at work but publishes no hours or formal tour. The guide therefore promises no yard entry, builder interaction, active work at a particular time or tool handling.

Both guides use exact, real and credited photography. The Appenzell image is an official 2025 photograph by Roger Oberholzer that shows visitors above the active production floor. The Ajman image is Sarah Dea's 2014 documentary portrait of Ali Mattar Al Shamsi in his boatbuilding yard for The National. The Ajman media boundary makes the date and identity explicit and does not treat the photograph as evidence of current access, personnel or working conditions. Both images are locally served and clickable. Reuse rights have not been independently cleared, and attribution is not treated as permission.

## Held after review

The exact official images reviewed are dated, weak at hero scale or overly staged for the current EA visual standard. The access evidence remains recorded, but the candidate is not published.
- Candidate 2, Sinjska Alka, remains on calendar hold because the official 2026 event took place from 7 to 9 August. The museum may later become a separately evaluated related alternative, but it does not resolve the live-event premise.
- Candidate 89, a Rio samba-school rehearsal, remains on calendar hold. The current route found in this review documents the completed 2026 rehearsal season rather than a future public date.

The official Visit Ajman detail page currently attaches an unrelated hiking-safety image to the Dhow Yard entry. EA does not repeat that error. The guide uses a precisely credited documentary boatyard image and states its age and limits.
