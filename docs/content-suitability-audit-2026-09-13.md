# Content suitability audit

13 September 2026. Scope: all 72 public guides, their headlines, summaries, participation framing and responsibility notes.

## Standard applied

EA can be surprising without being marginal, polarising or physically extreme. A guide should offer a clear public route, a credible reason to be there and enough practical context for an ordinary visitor to decide. Novelty alone is not a selection reason. Headlines should not rely on danger, shock, conquest, exclusivity, objectification or exaggerated transformation.

Recognisable reference experiences remain important when EA can reveal the work, timing or context behind them. Less familiar experiences remain valuable when they are publicly accessible, locally rooted and easy to understand without insider status.

## Findings

- No public headline or summary uses extreme, dangerous, crazy, adrenaline, bucket-list, once-in-a-lifetime, untouched, primitive or exotic framing.
- Alcohol-related guides centre production, food or social context. Tasting is optional where relevant, adult and driving boundaries are explicit, and no guide encourages volume or intoxication.
- Animal-related guides keep visitors outside working routes, prohibit contact unless explicitly offered, avoid guaranteed sightings and state where EA has not assessed welfare.
- Religious and ceremonial settings are framed as public access with quiet conduct, photography and participation limits. No spiritual result or insider belonging is promised.
- Thermal, water and outdoor guides state environmental and capability limits. Optional lake or winter swimming in Kuopio had become too dominant in the title, so the public title and summary now centre the full smoke-sauna evening. Swimming remains clearly optional in the practical detail.
- No guide grants access to a private home, sacred knowledge, protected location or closed working area. The current catalogue contains no locally validated EA Selected claim.

## Result

No guide was removed in this pass. The 72-guide public catalogue remains suitable under the restrained EA position, with one Kuopio framing correction. The London additions were screened under the same standard and rely on bounded public tours rather than independent access to working or closed areas. This is an editorial risk screen, not field validation, legal advice, a safety audit or proof that every visitor will find every subject uncontroversial. Reapply the screen to each new batch and during source rechecks.
