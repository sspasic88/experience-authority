# Destination depth and daily discovery

Release scope: 13 September 2026.

## What changed

- 59 public-source guides across the existing 36 countries and territories. Four new guides deepen Porto, Kyoto and Venice rather than opening more single-entry countries.
- Porto and Gaia now have four guides across three visitor interests. Kyoto has four guides across three interests. Venice has two hands-on guides spanning making and movement.
- Six authored local planning chapters cover Porto and Gaia, Kyoto, Istanbul, Mexico City and Xochimilco, Singapore, and Venice. Each explains the connection, suggested pace, timing constraints and separate booking/access requirements. Country and region membership is tested. These are not geocoded routes, packaged tours or live feasibility checks.
- A single action saves a chapter to the device-local Passport. Existing saved stages, comparison choices and the trip draft are preserved. Visitors still choose their own days in the planner.
- Daily discovery has fourteen dated, individually written angles from 13 to 26 September. It serves the current available edition, permits permanent dated sharing and keeps earlier editions accessible. Future dates, malformed dates and missing guide editions are not public. If the series is not extended, the last available edition remains explicitly dated as an archive entry rather than silently looping or inventing a fresh publication.
- The new-publications page uses an explicit release log, independent of source-check dates. The homepage draws its recent guides from a thirty-day release window. Older undated guides are not assigned invented publication dates.
- Homepage and guide-detail links connect discovery to city-level planning. City pages keep filters and photographs before the longer chapter, with a direct jump to the planning idea.
- Dated sharing gets the correct experience photograph and summary. Query-date pages canonicalise to the base discovery page and are noindex. The current base page and release log enter the sitemap only when there is eligible content.

## Content decisions

Published after individual public-source review:

1. Clérigos organ programme, Porto. The standing noon programme is distinguished from a potentially stale month-specific heading. Concert and tower access are not conflated.
2. Bolhão market, Porto. EA proposes an independent ingredient-led visit, not a sold tour, guaranteed tasting or assumption of local provenance.
3. Kunjyukan Koh-labo, Kyoto. The free permanent scent gallery is distinguished from factory tours, ceremonies and separately booked programmes.
4. Huang Xiaozhe lampworking, Venice. A contemporary Castello atelier, not a Murano factory or an invented family lineage. Beginner outcomes and cooling/collection remain bounded.

Vienna's strudel demonstration and Barcelona's trencadís workshop remain deferred in this pass. The examined photographs were below the desired publication quality. No low-resolution placeholders or generated photographs were substituted.

All four published images are real official-source photographs with direct lower-right source credits, accurate alt text, source/depiction records and explicit uncleared-reuse status. Credit is not presented as a licence. The named Clérigos photographer is retained. No photography permission request, booking, purchase or provider message was sent.

These are destination-depth additions, not blanket approvals of the original 200 candidates. The original candidate workbook and its review decisions were not bulk-modified.

## Measurable scope and remaining work

- Guides with a same-area or explicitly connected-area continuation: 19 of 55 before, 24 of 59 now.
- Countries with only one guide: 26 of 36 before, 25 of 36 now. Most geographic coverage remains shallow. A four-guide city is not a comprehensive destination service.
- There is now a concrete daily editorial product, a publication log and a direct path into retained plans. These features do not establish a measured increase in retention. Assess consenting visitor cohorts and return visits after release, not a subjective numerical score.
- Extend the daily editorial queue before 27 September. Prefer fresh discoveries, substantially developed local coverage and useful revisits over an endless recycled rotation. New research must still pass the publication gate.
- Continue priority depth in recognisable cities, with genuine contrasts in food, craft, movement, culture and nature. Do not add a category just to display a count.
- The trip remains device-local, not a cloud account or end-to-end booking system. No notifications, subscriptions, provider partnerships or transactional integrations were introduced.
