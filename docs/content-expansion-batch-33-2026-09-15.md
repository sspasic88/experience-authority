## Published guides

- Candidate 41 becomes **Meet the rose before it becomes water** on Jabal Akhdar. Current official and host pages support a seasonal farm visit with rose-picking and distillation context. The guide does not promise bloom, unrestricted terrace access or participation beyond the host's instructions.
- Candidate 33 becomes **Walk inside the earthen ring** in Yongding. The original stay and shared household meal are replaced by a ticketed public heritage route. Private rooms, resident access and domestic life remain outside the promise.
- Candidate 129 becomes **Follow the pearl back underwater** in Bahrain. The current public route requires a BTEA-approved licensed operator and keeps beginner snorkelling distinct from certified diving. A pearl find and oyster retention are not promised.
- Candidate 132 becomes **Read a city from the waterline** in Kampong Ayer. The public water-taxi, marked walkway and gallery route replaces the unsupported resident-led stay premise. Homes and private jetties are not visitor space.
- Candidate 67 becomes **Let the delta decide the route** in the Okavango. The guide is a licensed, professionally guided passenger ride. It does not claim a community-control model for an unnamed operator, allow self-poling or promise wildlife.
