## Sources, access and photography

- The Living Museum of Obwarzanek currently describes a roughly one-hour interactive workshop with individual dough shaping, limited places and advance booking. Its FAQ and terms differ on whether the workshop object is edible. EA preserves that conflict and directs visitors to buy a normally produced obwarzanek separately if tasting matters.
- muWi currently offers a guided visit combined with a shorter or standard stained-glass workshop. The visitor works with glass, copper and solder under instruction. The published age threshold is 13. Language, mobility, ventilation and other current safety conditions remain a direct pre-booking check.

The adult beginner master series is marked full and is intended for people who have entered or plan to enter the competition. EA does not present it as a casual visitor class.

## Broad-appeal suitability

This release applies the same non-extreme, non-polarising filter to new and existing guides. Surprise must come from a revealing local process or a stronger way of seeing a familiar place, not from danger, controversy, humiliation, illegality, voyeurism or shock value. The current catalogue contains no visitor invitation to weapons, illegal access, extreme endurance, political campaigning, religious conversion or unbounded contact with private communities.

Existing animal, alcohol, bathing, religious-site, wilderness and working-environment guides remain only where the public role is ordinary and clearly bounded. They carry specific age, consent, welfare, clothing, weather, sobriety, access or host-direction notes where relevant. Their presence is not an endorsement for every visitor. This was an editorial desk review, not legal, medical, accessibility or local-cultural certification.
