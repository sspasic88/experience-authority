# Content expansion batch 23

13 September 2026

## Published

Three original-register candidates were narrowed into public-source guides:

- Candidate 25 becomes a full-day halloumi route into the Troodos foothills. The public promise is hands-on halloumi and anari making, brunch and variable food stops under the provider's current route. It is not a standalone drop-in class or unrestricted producer access.
- Candidate 17 becomes a prebooked walk through Mooska smoke-sauna farm. It introduces three saunas, construction, heating and family tradition, with the listed smoked-meat tasting. It does not promise bathing, private ritual access or entry to every building.
- Candidate 81 becomes a one-hour Arte Añil workshop in Suchitoto. The visitor works with the supplied scarf or T-shirt and current workshop materials. It does not establish authority over all Salvadoran indigo traditions or permit dyeing personal garments.

All three guides use real source-specific photography. Each image is clickable and carries the standard `Image: creator / source ↗` credit. The halloumi and indigo images come from the named operators. The Mooska image comes from Visit Estonia and credits Silver Gutmann. Their records state that publication-source attribution does not independently clear reuse rights or prove an EA visit.

## Held

Candidate 200, the Norf'k language class on Norfolk Island, has a credible current public access route and useful language context. It was not published in this batch because the available reviewed photography showed the island or a separate celebration rather than the class or a defensible learning context. A generic destination photograph would weaken EA's visual and evidentiary standard.
