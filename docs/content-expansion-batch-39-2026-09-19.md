# Content expansion batch 39

Date: 19 September 2026

## Outcome

Five original research candidates now have bounded public-source guides. The authored catalogue reaches 154 guides across 104 countries and territories. The reconciliation maps 115 of the original 200 candidates through 72 narrowed public scopes, 41 related alternatives and two protected public programmes.

No guide receives EA Selected status, local validation, provider endorsement, exclusive access or an implied EA visit. One expired date-led guide remains outside the live public set, leaving 153 current ways in.

## Decisions

### Candidate 44: Panauti Community Homestay

Published as `Stay where the town knows your host`, a narrowed public scope. Community Homestay Network currently offers an assigned household, accommodation, meals and optional hosted additions inside a women-led Panauti network. The guide does not promise one household, personal intimacy, uniform facilities or every listed activity.

Primary source: https://communityhomestay.com/homestays/panauti

### Candidate 87: Chinchero weaving

Published as `Follow the colour back to the wool`, a related alternative. CTTC currently publishes an artisan-run demonstration that connects spinning, natural dyeing and backstrap weaving. The public evidence supports observation and direct purchase, not the original hands-on weaving and dyeing promise.

Primary source: https://www.textilescusco.org/special-activity-detail/weaving-demostration/3

### Candidate 188: Niue uga encounter

Published as `Meet the island through the uga`, a related alternative. The original Taue night format remains publicly listed, but A5 provides the clearer current schedule, booking route and exact high-quality official imagery. The guide therefore names the substitution rather than blending two operators or inventing night access.

Primary source: https://www.niueisland.com/all-tours/a5-tours

### Candidate 142: Mindelo live music

Published as `Let the room introduce Mindelo`, a related alternative. Le Metalo currently operates as a public music restaurant with a changing live programme. The guide deliberately lets the current bill define the genre and never promises that every night is morna.

Primary source: https://lemetalo.cv/

### Candidate 40: Wadi Rum overnight

Published as `Stay until the desert loses its colour`, a narrowed public scope. Real Bedouin Experience publishes one named camp stay with transfer, dinner, breakfast, tea and accommodation. The guide does not generalise one commercial camp into all Bedouin life or guarantee stars, weather or unrestricted protected-area access.

Primary source: https://www.wadirum-jordan.com/overnight-stay.html
