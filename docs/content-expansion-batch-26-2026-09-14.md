# Content expansion batch 26

## Editorial direction

This batch pairs immediate geographic recognition with a less familiar EA entry point. Monument Valley begins with Navajo-managed access rather than the repeated road view. Jatiluwih begins with water and shared infrastructure rather than scenery alone. Pinglin turns a familiar drink back into a seasonal leaf, host instruction and partial making process.

All three remain independent public-source guides. None is EA Selected, locally validated, provider-ranked or booked by EA.

## Published guides

- `read-monument-valley-beyond-the-scenic-drive` maps original candidate 75 only as a protected public programme. The visitor must choose an operator currently listed by Navajo Nation Parks and Recreation. The guide grants no wider route, story, recording or cultural access.
- `follow-balis-water-before-the-rice` narrows original candidate 47 to the farmer-led public trek listed by Jatiluwih tourism village. It does not open every field, channel, temple, ritual or community decision.
- `make-tea-before-taipei-drinks-it` narrows original candidate 53 to an advance reservation at Green Light Tea Plantation. The farm confirms the current season and production steps. A full commercial cycle, fixed take-home result or every variety is not promised.

## Photography and credit

The Monument Valley hero uses the official lead image from one currently listed backcountry operator. Its unusual Big Hogan frame deliberately avoids another generic highway view. Reuse rights have not been independently cleared.

Jatiluwih uses Jorge Franganillo's licensed CC BY 2.0 photograph. The frame keeps flooded foreground, terrace edges, shelters and weather visible so the image reads as agriculture and infrastructure rather than an isolated panorama.

Pinglin uses a current 2026 New Taipei City Travel photograph from a separate low-carbon tea route. The media boundary explicitly says that it does not depict Green Light Tea Plantation or prove that farm's current format. Reuse rights have not been independently cleared.

Every image opens its exact source from the lower-right credit and retains a separate depiction and rights record.
