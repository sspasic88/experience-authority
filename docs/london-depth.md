## Why this city and this sequence

London was an obvious relatability gap. The selection starts with Borough Market because a first-time visitor can place it immediately. The guide then changes the question from what to eat to how ingredients, traders and knowledge make the market work. The Royal Opera House moves behind another familiar public image and centres rehearsal, costume and production. Hidden London completes the sequence by revealing earlier city layers beside a transport system people use every day.

The chapter does not force all three into one itinerary. The market currently uses selected Friday mornings. The theatre and transport routes are separate timed bookings, and the Underground programme has substantial footwear, mobility and confined-space requirements. The page therefore recommends one anchor per day and states that there is no continuous route, combined ticket, transfer or package.
