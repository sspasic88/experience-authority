## Editorial boundaries

The public guide always stays narrower than the original candidate where evidence requires it. Museum visits do not become promised live performances. Observation does not become hands-on participation. A public city walk does not become a private sharing circle. Wildlife is never guaranteed. The Djibouti guide requires verification of a current authorised operator and treats the official decree as the controlling boundary.

All ten guides use real, locally served photographs with a direct source credit, rights status, visual review and depiction boundary. No AI-generated photograph is used. A source photograph can establish visual context, but it does not prove EA attendance, current delivery or provider endorsement.

## Visitor value

The ten guides are connected to Compass search, participation and time filters, country and regional pages, the new-publication log, editorial pathways, Journey planning and sharing. Four new hand-composed homepage editions balance recognisable entry points with less expected discoveries. The Compass opening now gives the new material deliberate visibility instead of leaving it behind implementation order.

Three additional daily discoveries extend the finite authored queue through 18 December 2026. The other seven guides are available immediately through the catalogue and release surfaces. They are not scheduled beyond the 90-day source-review boundary merely to make the daily feed look longer.
